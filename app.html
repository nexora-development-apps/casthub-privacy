CastHub

Cast videos, music and photos from your Android phone to your TV.

CastHub sends the video you are watching on your phone to the big screen in one tap. It is not screen mirroring, so your phone stays free while the video plays on your TV.

Works with

  • Roku TVs and players (with the free CastHub channel)
  • Chromecast and Google TV
  • Smart TVs that support DLNA

How to cast to Roku

  1. Install CastHub on your Android phone.
  2. Add the CastHub channel on your Roku and open it.
  3. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi network as your Roku.
  4. Play a video in CastHub and tap the cast button.

Features

  • Video in MP4, HLS, DASH and MKV formats
  • Music and photo casting
  • Subtitles (SRT and VTT)
  • Play, pause, seek and stop from your phone
  • Built-in browser and ad blocker
